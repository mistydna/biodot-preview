Join us

We welcome students and researchers who want to use data to understand ALS and other neurodegenerative diseases.

Ways to join

PhD students

Funded PhD projects are advertised through King's doctoral training programmes. If you're applying for a studentship or fellowship and want to work with us, get in touch early so we can shape a project together.

MSc and BSc projects

We host research projects for King's MSc and BSc students each year. Contact us before the project allocation period to talk about ideas.

Postdocs and fellows

We're happy to support fellowship applications from researchers whose interests fit ours. Send a CV and a short note on what you'd like to work on.

Get in touch

Email Ahmad at Ahmad.al_khleifat@kcl.ac.uk with your CV and a few lines on your interests.
