News

New papers, talks, awards and what's happening in the lab.

  • October 2026

    Our new website is live

    Welcome to the BIODOT Lab website. Here you'll find our team, our research and the latest from the lab.

  • 31 March 2026

    Project MinE finds a genetic cause for 1 in 4 people with MND

    The largest rare variant analysis of MND to date, from the Project MinE consortium, analysed DNA from more than 18,000 people and was published in Nature Genetics.

  • July 2024

    Visit to the University of Jordan

    Ahmad visited Amman to share our work and build collaborations to understand ALS risk worldwide.

  • 8 June 2022

    Ahmad receives the ENCALS Young Investigator Award

    The European Network to Cure ALS award recognises outstanding ALS researchers under 35. It was presented at the ENCALS Meeting in Edinburgh.

  • 22 February 2022

    MND Association and ALS Association fellowships awarded

    Ahmad was awarded the MND Association Lady Edith Wolfson Fellowship and the ALS Association's Milton Safenowitz Fellowship to carry out large-scale genetic analysis in ALS.
