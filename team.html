Principal Investigator

Dr Ahmad Al Khleifat

MB BCh, MSc, PhD

Senior Research Fellow, King's College London

Ahmad is a clinician-scientist working on the genetics of amyotrophic lateral sclerosis (ALS). He holds the MND Association Lady Edith Wolfson Senior Fellowship and the ALS Association's Milton Safenowitz Senior Fellowship. He trained in medicine in Egypt, then completed his MSc and PhD in Clinical Neuroscience at King's College London, working with Professors Ammar Al-Chalabi and John Powell at the Maurice Wohl Clinical Neuroscience Institute.

His group uses big data for precision medicine: identifying disease genes from whole genome sequencing, developing protein-based biomarkers for neurodegeneration, and applying AI to drug discovery and clinical trial design. His work on structural variation in 6,500 ALS genomes found that variants in VCP and ERBB4, alongside the C9orf72 expansion, are linked to ALS risk and to how the disease presents.

Roles and awards

  • Chair of the Trans-Ancestral Genetics working group in Project MinE, which he created to bring people of all ancestries into ALS genetics research
  • Lead of the Drug Discovery and Trials Optimisation working group in the DEMON network
  • Co-founder of the Alan Turing Institute Interest Group in Precision Dementia Medicine, and of the Alzheimer's Association group on AI for Precision Dementia Medicine
  • EDI Theme Representative, NIHR Maudsley Biomedical Research Centre
  • ENCALS Young Investigator Award, 2022
